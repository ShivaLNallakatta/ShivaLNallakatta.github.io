About

Data platforms people can trust

I have spent more than 20 years building data platforms, including more than seven years as a Senior Data Engineer at Microsoft. There I architected near-petabyte data services for Azure Commerce, kept critical financial data streams above 99.9% availability, and maintained 100% SOX compliance across five cloud environments, including secure government clouds.

Today at Simetric, an IoT connectivity platform, I lead the move from SQL Server to a Databricks and open-source Unity Catalog lakehouse. I design ingestion from dozens of carrier connectivity platforms, each with its own schema and delivery method.
